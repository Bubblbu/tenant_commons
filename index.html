Tenant Commons

Hi, I'm Asura, the developer behind Tenant Commons. This is a work in progress — buy me a coffee if you want to help keep it going.

Try to find out who owns your building, and what else they own, and you hit a wall. Numbered companies, nested holdings, and law-office addresses hide the trail. City records track purchases, assessments, and taxes. They say nothing about who a tenant is actually dealing with. So tenant research rarely adds up. One tenant traces a corporate chain and learns something real, then the next tenant in the same building starts from zero.

Tenant Commons is a shared reference for that knowledge. It combines persistent building and unit identifiers with public ownership and assessment records. Eventually it will also let tenants record and share what they find, like two shell companies turning out to be the same landlord, so research adds up instead of resetting.

This map is not the Tenant Commons. It's a small, early view into it: a public snapshot of what the data currently knows. The claims and curation system, and deeper ownership research, are still being built.

Status: early prototype, actively in development. Data is incomplete and will have gaps and errors. Treat everything here as a starting point, not a verified record.

Tenant Commons is open source. The code is on GitHub, if you want to look under the hood or contribute.
